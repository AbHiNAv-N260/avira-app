Understanding your Body

What is Vaginal pH?

The pH Scale

Vaginal pH is a measure of acidity that maintains a healthy environment for intimate health.

Why it Matters

A healthy range is typically between 3.8 and 4.5. This balance is essential for natural defense.

When to Seek Help

Tracking your pH helps you notice changes and understand when professional medical evaluation may be needed.

AVIRA is a screening-support tool, not a diagnostic device. pH alone cannot identify the cause of an imbalance. Further medical evaluation may be required for a definitive diagnosis.
